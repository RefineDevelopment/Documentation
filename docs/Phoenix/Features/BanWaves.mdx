# Ban waves

A ban wave is a set of punishments staged over time and released together in one
go. Because everyone is banned in the same moment, a cheater cannot work out
which of their accounts was detected when, or which check caught them — which is
the point of banning in waves rather than one at a time.

Waves are staged with `/banwave add`, held until someone releases them, and can be
scheduled to go out on their own.

## Lifecycle

1. **Create** a wave with a name.
2. **Stage** accounts into it with `/banwave add`, which records the punishment
   exactly as if you had issued it — reason, ladder, duration.
3. **Review** what is queued with `/banwave view`.
4. **Release** it, either manually with `/banwave release` or automatically at a
   scheduled time.
5. **Void** it afterwards if the wave turns out to have been wrong.

A wave that has not gone out yet can be thrown away with `/banwave cancel`. Once
released, `/banwave void` is the way back — it reverses the punishments the wave
issued, with a reason.

:::warning Releasing is not reversible on its own
`/banwave release` punishes everyone staged in the wave across the network at
once, and asks for confirmation first. Undoing it means `/banwave void`, which is
a separate permission.
:::

## Permissions

Every subcommand has its own node, so staging a wave and releasing one can be
handed to different ranks. In the shipped preset, staging sits at SrMod and
releasing at Admin.

Command | Permission | Description
------- | ---------- | -----------
`/banwave create <name>` | `core.command.banwave.create` | Start a new wave.
`/banwave add <player> ...` | `core.command.banwave.add` | Stage an account in a wave.
`/banwave remove <wave> <player>` | `core.command.banwave.remove` | Take an account back out of a wave.
`/banwave list` | `core.command.banwave.list` | The waves still waiting.
`/banwave view <wave>` | `core.command.banwave.view` | What is staged in a wave.
`/banwave schedule <wave> ...` | `core.command.banwave.schedule` | Set the day and time a wave goes out.
`/banwave unschedule <wave>` | `core.command.banwave.unschedule` | Stop a wave going out on its own.
`/banwave release <wave>` | `core.command.banwave.release` | Send a wave out now.
`/banwave void <wave> <reason>` | `core.command.banwave.void` | Take back a wave that has already gone out.
`/banwave cancel <wave>` | `core.command.banwave.cancel` | Throw a wave away without releasing it.

`core.command.banwave` is required for all of them. Aliases: `/wave`,
`/punishwave`.

:::note Staging respects your own limits
`/banwave add` runs the same checks as issuing the punishment directly — you
cannot stage a punishment you would not be allowed to hand out yourself.
:::
