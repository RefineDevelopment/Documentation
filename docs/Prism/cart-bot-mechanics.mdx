# Cart bot

Cart practices bow-driven minecart sequences, cart spacing, and recovery between burst attacks.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Spawn | `/spawnbot cart medium` |
| Configuration | `plugins/Prism/bots/cart.yml` |
| Practice focus | TNT minecart placement and burst damage |
| Controls | Shift + right-click the bot |

:::info Configuration paths
Use `bots/cart.yml` for Cart settings. Technique/ruleset controls remain under `CART-BOT`; reaction, prediction, healing, mending, and cooldown tuning is grouped under `ADVANCED.CART`. See [Configuration](bot-configuration.mdx#advanced-settings).
:::

The Cart bot is a Cart PvPer first. Cart Bait, Instacart and Chain Cart are its
offence; the sword is a short utility bridge that creates the next Cart window.
Sustained sword play only happens when Carting is actually impossible.

## Cart sequences

| Technique | Execution |
| --- | --- |
| Pre-cart | Rail, cart, draw Flame bow, fire at the cart |
| Pre-rail | Rail, draw and fire Flame bow, place cart into the arrow path |
| Instacart | Draw and fire Flame bow, rail, cart |
| Pearl cart | Throw pearl, draw bow during travel, wait for landing, solve a reachable placement, instacart |
| Block cart | Place cover, jump, draw and fire, rail, cart |
| Parry cart | Use an observed upward knockback window for an instacart; requires Expert difficulty and sufficient health |
| Chain cart | Follow observed damage with another bow-driven instacart |
| Expo | Load an arrow crossbow if necessary, rail, cart, adjacent fire, shoot through fire toward the cart's lower half |

Arrow-first techniques issue the rail/cart placement inputs immediately after
releasing the arrow within the same server tick. Each placement still uses the
normal server interaction path and rechecks reach, line of sight, and inventory.
Bow charge, Flame, projectile speed, ammunition consumption, and explosion
behavior come from the server. Plain bows and flint alone do not qualify as a
bow-cart setup. Expo requires a fire source and an arrow-loaded crossbow or loose
arrows to load one. The existing eight-block Expo spacing requirement is checked
throughout execution.

## Cart identity

- Every melee selection carries a reason: `ANTI_RUSH`, `CREATE_SPACING`,
  `BAIT_CHASE`, `CART_COOLDOWN_BRIDGE`, `EMERGENCY`, `FINISH_WINDOW`,
  `NO_VALID_CART` or `MELEE_MODE`. A proposed swing with no reason is replaced by
  the best open Cart line, a Cart Bait, or Cart spacing.
- Tactical reasons (anti-rush, spacing, bait chase, cooldown bridge) share a
  budget of two server-confirmed hits, or three swings that never connected.
  After that the bot re-plans toward Cart. The budget resets when a Cart action
  starts, when a decision is made at Cart spacing, when a true emergency ends,
  after a heal, and after 40 ticks of failed spacing against a glued opponent.
- Emergencies (high threat, a fresh pop, being comboed or boxed in) keep full
  melee rights. So do missing Cart resources, three in-reach decisions with no
  legal rail cell, carts that cannot reach the hotbar, and Melee Pressure mode.
- Outside sword reach, melee is never a chase while Cart intent is active. The
  bot instead creates Cart geometry: `CREATE_CART_DISTANCE` (step back to about
  4.5 blocks), `BAIT_TARGET_FORWARD` (walk back to about 6.5 blocks while a
  chaser closes), `CREATE_RAIL_ANGLE` (approach to about 4.2 blocks with wider
  strafing), or `CREATE_DETONATION_LOS`. Backing off never sprints, and every
  intent settles at a Cart distance, so spacing cannot turn into running.
- A Cart Bait held against a chaser stays drawn for up to one extra second.
  Once a rail cell is in reach, it becomes a real Instacart that keeps its bow
  charge. If the opponent is already in sword reach, the bait is dropped.
- A completed cart holds spacing for six ticks through the detonation, then
  re-plans. Missed, rejected or aborted techniques re-plan immediately, or when
  the short cart cooldown clears.
- A feinted Cart Bait is not repeated within two seconds. The fallback is
  another Cart line or Cart spacing, not a sword phase.

## Combat and recovery

- Melee remains available between tactical decisions under the Cart identity rules above.
- A safe healing window at or below the configured preferred health takes
  priority over an ordinary cart attempt. The default is 14 health, or seven
  hearts. A clear melee finish can still take priority.
- A low-health bot does not pearl-chase merely because the opponent popped.
- After escaping to a safe healing window, a recent totem pop no longer triggers
  another backward pearl instead of healing.
- Knockback alone does not discard an active cart sequence. The next interaction
  still rechecks reach, geometry, and health; totem pops still interrupt it.
- Food and potion timing stays with the existing recovery controllers.
- Safe inventory windows refill up to two empty cart hotbar slots from carried
  reserves. Existing items are preserved.
- Completing a placement sequence does not invent an opponent-damage event.
  Follow-up opportunities depend on observed combat events.

## Verification

Run `.\gradlew.bat build --console=plain` for compilation, the JUnit suite, and
plugin packaging.

:::warning Diagnostics are not recommended
Enabling console diagnostics spams your console with logs for every bot movement and technique. Leave diagnostics disabled during normal use.
:::

Keep `CART-BOT.DEBUG: false`. When enabled, it logs `[CART-IDENTITY]` (per decision),
`[CART-CONTINUATION]` (first decision after a Cart action ends, misses, or a
heal completes) and `[CART-MELEE-BUDGET]` (every utility swing). The always-on
`[CART-DIAGNOSTIC]` heartbeat also reports `cartIntent` and `meleeReason`.

Live Paper verification is still needed for projectile collision and combat feel:

1. Use a kit with a sword, Flame bow, arrows, rails, and two carts but no flint.
   Confirm melee pressure and bow-driven cart explosions.
2. Remove Flame or ammunition. Confirm the bot keeps fighting with its sword.
3. Chase and retreat to exercise cart openings; damage the bot to verify healing
   and escape choices.
4. Exercise pearl carting across open terrain and a blocked landing. Confirm no
   placement occurs before teleporting or outside reach.
5. Equip a loaded crossbow and fire source. Confirm Expo fires through actual
   fire, respects spacing, and consumes the carried projectile.

Placement damage values remain tactical estimates; the server determines actual
damage and whether an arrow hits. This update does not implement every player
technique in the reference guide, such as stacked-cart bursts, wall-pearl aiming,
or a complete shulker regear routine.
