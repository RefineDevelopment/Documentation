# Netherite Potion bot

Netherite Potion combines sword pressure with healing potions, recovery, and armor repair.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Spawn | `/spawnbot netheritepotion medium` |
| Configuration | `plugins/Prism/bots/netherite-potion.yml` |
| Practice focus | Potion combat, healing, and durability |
| Controls | Shift + right-click the bot |

The NetheritePotion bot uses `NetheritePotStrategy` for encounter decisions and
`NetheritePotMechanics` for vanilla attacks, item use, thrown potions, and XP
bottles. `NetheritePotPositioning` checks short escape routes.

New bots start at Medium (normal difficulty). The kit contains exactly two
totems: one in the offhand and one in the hotbar. Spare inventory slots contain
healing potions, and consumed totems are not replenished. The totem amount is
fixed for this kit. Melee attacks are capped at three blocks even when a custom
combat configuration requests a longer range.

## Fight phases

- **Early resource saving:** prefer a safe apple around 5–5.5 effective hearts.
  Emergency potions still take priority; a substantial armor deficit also
  permits earlier potion use.
- **Midgame aggression:** a completed repair with observed durability recovery
  starts resource conversion. Valuable pressure windows permit earlier potions
  and delayed apples while a spare totem is equipped. Keep three healing
  resources in reserve, except when survival requires spending them.
- **Durability control:** after two repairs, or with no XP remaining, three or
  fewer healing resources trigger conservative play. A durability lead suppresses
  ordinary critical jumps and risky distance punishment.

An attempted repair does not count as a completed mend. Repairs require Mending
armor and actual XP bottles, use the existing mend threshold, and stop after
sufficient recovery, exhausted XP, or a bounded timeout. The bot uses the existing
vitals controller for saturation regeneration and vanilla XP pickup.

## Recovery and punishment

Apple positioning considers separation, relative velocity, facing, attack cooldown,
cover, and knockback. Escape routes check body clearance, floor support, hazards,
loaded chunks, and the world border. An apple can continue behind cover. At critical
health, an available potion interrupts it. A healthy bot can cancel an apple to
attack an opponent who turns and runs; a cooldown prevents repeated feints.

An opponent's main-hand or offhand apple starts a punish sequence. It aims for
three confirmed critical hits below 100 ms ping, or two at higher ping, but shortens
the chain when the observed eating window is almost over. A falling sprint hit
then creates recovery space. Sprint knockback and critical hits use their distinct
vanilla sprint conditions; missed, cancelled, and blocked attacks do not advance
the chain. All attacks retain the configured reach, line-of-sight, and cooldown
checks.

The optional distance punish requires stable latency and velocity, level terrain,
a clear retreat, and sufficient health. It waits outside reach for an observed
swing, then tries an extra critical and the finisher. A timeout returns it to the
ordinary finisher. Repeated lateral movement uses grounded interception. A healing
potion held while looking down or turning away predicts a recovery opening.

High-health opponent apples create a temporary aggression window. Totems extend
pressure only with an equipped spare, sufficient resources, and a favorable
opening; the bot never forces a totem pop or manufactures healing. Self resources
are counted from inventory. Opponent decisions use exposed combat state and
equipped armor, without reading their storage inventory or assuming hidden supplies.

## In-game verification

:::warning Diagnostics are not recommended
Enabling console diagnostics spams your console with logs for every bot movement and technique. Leave diagnostics disabled during normal use.
:::

Keep the shared `debug-combat-memory` switch set to `false` in `config.yml`.

1. Fight with a normal netherite potion kit; observe early low-health apples and
   emergency healing when retreat is unsafe.
2. Start an apple in either hand, cancel it, and strafe away during the bot's own
   apple. Check bounded punish sequences and purposeful cancellation.
3. Repeat near walls, ledges, water, hazards, and the world border. Check that
   recovery does not deliberately enter an unchecked route.
4. Damage Mending armor and give the bot XP. Confirm bottle consumption, XP
   pickup, repaired durability, and the midgame transition.
5. Run down potions and totems, vary ping, and disable critical hits, auto-food,
   auto-totem, or auto-mend. Check that the corresponding behavior is gated.
6. For gap-timing practice, use sword, armor, apples, and XP without potions or
   totems. Use the existing replay recorder to review healing windows and trades;
   this change does not add an automatic replay analyst.

These scenarios require a live Paper server; a Gradle build verifies compilation
and packaging, not combat balance or latency behavior.
