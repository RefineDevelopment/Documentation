# Permissions

Prism uses two permission nodes. Players don't need any permission to spawn and control
their own bots.

## Nodes

### `prism.admin`

- **Default:** operators (Bukkit's default for undeclared nodes)
- **Grants:** `/prism`, `/prism version`, `/prism reload`, `/prism debug` and `/prism mlexport`.

### `prism.bot.admin`

- **Default:** `false`. Operators **don't** get it automatically.
- **Grants:** opening and editing the management, profile, equipment and inventory menus of
  bots owned by other players.

Without this node, Shift + Right-Click on someone else's bot does nothing, and
`MenuAPI.openManagement` and `MenuAPI.openProfile` return `INVALID_PLAYER_STATE`.

## What needs no permission

| Action | Who can do it |
| --- | --- |
| `/spawnbot` and all its subcommands | Any player |
| Controlling a bot you spawned | The bot's owner |
| Saving your own bot profile | Any player (when [profile storage](database.mdx) is enabled) |

To restrict bot spawning, block `/spawnbot` with your permissions or command-blocking plugin,
or cancel `BotSpawnEvent` through the [API](api.mdx#events).

:::info Staff bot controls
`prism.bot.admin` defaults to false. Give it to operators explicitly if they should manage other players' bots.
:::
