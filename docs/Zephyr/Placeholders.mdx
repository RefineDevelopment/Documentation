## Zephyr

These are the all placeholders Zephyr offers.

### Profile
Placeholder                   | Returns                           | Example
----------------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------
`%zephyr_profile_kills%`      | The player's kills.               | `273`
`%zephyr_profile_deaths%`     | The player's deaths.              | `24`
`%zephyr_profile_kdr%`        | The player's kills to death ratio | `11.375`
`%zephyr_profile_streak%`     | The player's current kill streak  | `5`
`%zephyr_profile_beststreak%` | The player's best kill streak     | `15`

### Arena

Placeholder                             | Returns                                       | Example
--------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------
`%zephyr_arena_name%`                   | The name of the current arena                 | `Flowers`
`%zephyr_arena_displayname%`            | The display name of the current arena         | `&dFlowers`
`%zephyr_arena_kit%`                    | The display name of the current arena's kit   | `&cPvP`
`%zephyr_arena_timer%`                  | The seconds until next arena change           | `900`
`%zephyr_arena_timer_seconds%`          | The seconds until next arena change           | `900`
`%zephyr_arena_timer_formatted%`        | The formatted time until the next arena change | `15m`
`%zephyr_arena_timer_mmss%`             | The time until the next arena change in MM:SS | `15:00`
